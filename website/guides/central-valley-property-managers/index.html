Email help for property managers in the Central Valley

For small property teams in Fresno, Clovis, Modesto, Merced, Visalia, Bakersfield and Stockton.

By Harleen Ghuman, founder of Clerova · November 2026

Clerova is built in California's Central Valley, for small property teams in places like Fresno, Clovis, Modesto, Merced, Visalia, Bakersfield and Stockton. If you manage a few dozen to a few hundred units here, a lot of your day is email. Here's what tends to fill that inbox, and how to keep up.

What fills a Central Valley property manager's inbox

  • Heat-wave AC calls. On 105-degree days, an AC that stops cooling is urgent, especially for older residents, babies or anyone with a health condition. Those requests should jump the line.
  • Winter heat and water heaters. Tule fog nights get cold, and "no heat" or "no hot water" emails come in bunches.
  • Spanish-language requests. Many residents write in Spanish. Answering in the language they wrote in avoids mix-ups.
  • Owner and vendor emails. Invoices, schedule confirmations and owner questions mixed in with resident requests.
  • The same questions every month. When rent is due, how to pay, office hours, trash day and parking.

A simple routine that works

  • Check the inbox at set times and handle anything urgent first.
  • Answer every new request the same day, even with a short "we've got it".
  • Write down your common answers once (rent due date, late fee, office hours, after-hours number) so everyone gives the same answer.
  • Track repairs on a board, not in the inbox.
